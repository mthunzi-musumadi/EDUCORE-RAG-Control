Educore Pre-Rollout AI Audit Strategy & Discovery Survey
Phase 1 Discovery Framework & Departmental Audit Instrument
1. Executive Audit Overview & 3-Vector Methodology
This document establishes the Phase 1 (Days 1–30) discovery framework required under ISO/IEC 42001:2023 Clause 4.3 and Annex A.4.4. It outlines a 3-vector assessment methodology to audit current AI usage, identify data sensitivity risks, and populate the official AI System Inventory:
Vector 1: Technical & Network Traffic Discovery — Firewall, DNS, and CASB log traffic inspection for hits on commercial AI endpoints (api.openai.com, chatgpt.com, gemini.google.com). Financial credit card expense reviews.
Vector 2: Data Sensitivity & SharePoint Scoping — Automated Microsoft Purview Information Protection scans across SharePoint Online libraries using Zambian NRC regex matching and financial/pastoral risk keywords.
Vector 3: Departmental Survey & Risk Mapping — Deployment of the 10-Question Discovery Survey instrument across Finance, Procurement, IT, HR, Admin, and Faculty.
2. Departmental 10-Question Discovery Survey Instrument
This survey instrument captures operational AI usage across all business and academic units:
1. Current AI Tools Used: List all AI platforms (ChatGPT, Gemini, Copilot, Claude, Canva, Grammarly) accessed for work.
2. Account & Licensing Type: Indicate whether tools are accessed via personal email logins or official Educore M365 SSO.
3. Data Input Sensitivity: Select data types entered into AI tools (financial spreadsheets, payroll, student PII, lesson plans, code).
4. SharePoint Integration: Do you upload documents directly from SharePoint Online or OneDrive into external AI tools?
5. Hardware Access Points: Indicate devices used (Educore laptops, personal home PCs, smartphones).
6. Primary Workflows: Detail specific operational tasks automated using AI (summarization, formatting, quiz design, coding).
7. Student AI Observations: Have you observed students submitting AI-generated work, and how is it currently verified?
8. Unsanctioned Scripts & APIs: Are you using custom API keys, browser extensions, or unvetted software with embedded AI?
9. Purview Label Awareness: Are you familiar with Educore's data classification labels (Confidential - Admin/Finance, Internal - Educational)?
10. Training & Enterprise Needs: What specific training or approved enterprise M365/Azure tools would most improve your productivity?
