Educore AI Framework Implementation Handbook
Master Governing Authority for Enterprise Operations & Academic Learning
1. Executive Summary & Dual-Track Organizational Charter
This Master Implementation Handbook serves as the central governing authority for Artificial Intelligence (AI) integration across Educore Services Limited. It establishes a certifiable Artificial Intelligence Management System (AIMS) aligned with ISO/IEC 42001:2023, the EU AI Act, and the Zambian Data Protection Act (No. 3 of 2021). The framework operates across two distinct operational tracks:
Track 1: Enterprise Business Operations — Governing Finance, Procurement, IT/DevOps, HR, Legal, Facilities, and Operations. Focuses on data protection, financial accuracy, software vendor vetting, and secret-scanning.
Track 2: Academic & Pedagogical Transformation — Governing Faculty, Curriculum Leads, Teacher Academies, and Students across Sentinel Kabitaka, Trident, and Frontier. Focuses on human-in-the-loop review, Adelaide-model intellectual ownership declarations, and Viva Voce oral defenses.
2. Microsoft Cloud Architecture & Purview Container Security
Educore leverages its existing Microsoft 365, SharePoint Online, and Entra ID (Azure AD) environment, hosting custom RAG agents on Azure Cloud Services governed by Microsoft Purview Data Loss Prevention (DLP):
Confidential - Admin / Finance: M365 Copilot Enterprise for central administration, HR, and legal operations.
Confidential - Finance: M365 Copilot Finance with dual-key calculation audits for bursars and accounting staff.
Restricted - IT / Systems: GitHub Copilot Enterprise with pre-commit git hooks and automated secret scanning for IT engineers.
Internal - Educational: Azure OpenAI lesson design tools for faculty, operating under mandatory Human-in-the-Loop review.
Public / Educational: Azure AI Search Socratic tutors providing diagnostic hints for students without direct answer dumping.
3. 8 Departmental Edge-Case & Functional Non-Software Guardrails
The handbook codifies explicit non-software guardrails to mitigate risks that network firewalls cannot catch:
Finance: Dual-key independent calculation audits; anonymization of student NRCs and FQM mining subsidy data.
Procurement: Pre-procurement Vendor AI Addendums certifying zero data retention; air-gapped tender evaluations.
IT / DevOps: Git pre-commit secret scanning; mandatory SAST & Senior Engineer peer review sign-offs.
HR & Personnel: Absolute prohibition on automated candidate rejection; mandatory statutory DPIAs under Act No. 3 of 2021.
Faculty: Prohibition of automated summative exam grading; mandatory syllabus alignment verification.
Students (Tier C): Adelaide-model Declaration of Intellectual Ownership; process-based Viva Voce oral defenses.
Facilities & Security: EU AI Act Article 5 prohibition on facial recognition/emotion tracking; human emergency ticket dispatch.
Legal & Executive: Statutory verification against official Zambian law books; non-delegable executive accountability.
4. ISO 42001 Statement of Applicability & 6-Step AIIA SOP
The handbook incorporates all 38 ISO/IEC 42001:2023 Annex A reference controls and mandates the 6-Step AI System Impact Assessment (AIIA) workflow before deploying any new AI tool:
AIIA 6-Step Sequence: 1. Trigger Screening -> 2. Data Provenance & Purview Review -> 3. Rights & Harm Mapping -> 4. Safeguard & DLP Stop Conditions -> 5. Unanimous Steering Approval -> 6. Risk Registration & 12+ Month Purview Event Logging.
5. Governance Incident SLA
All AI queries, security incidents, or policy intake reports are routed to ai-governance@educore.ac.zm, with a mandatory 24-hour intake SLA and a 5-day formal resolution SLA.
