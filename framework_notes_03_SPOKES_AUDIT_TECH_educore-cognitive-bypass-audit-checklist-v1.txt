--- PARAGRAPHS ---
Educore Academic Cognitive Bypass Audit Checklist
Internal Quality Verification Matrix for Process-Based Learning
1. Operational Purpose & 4-Vector Audit Structure
This checklist provides internal auditors, campus principals, and department heads with a quantitative verification matrix to audit whether AI is supporting genuine student learning or causing cognitive bypass. It operates across 4 verification vectors:
Vector 1: Artifact Sampling — Comparing submitted coursework against raw student prompt logs and draft histories.
Vector 2: System Guardrails — Auditing Azure Socratic RAG bot logs in Microsoft Purview to verify diagnostic hint enforcement.
Vector 3: Viva Voce Oral Defenses — Conducting 3-minute unassisted oral spot-checks where students explain their submitted reasoning.
Vector 4: Gradebook Variance Benchmarking — Comparing take-home AI-assisted assignment marks against unassisted in-class exam baselines.
